# Licence

This repository contains model weights trained on two datasets with different terms.

1. `ixi/**` — trained on the IXI dataset (https://brain-development.org/ixi-dataset/), released under the Creative
   Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 licence (CC BY-SA 3.0). Weights, figures and metrics in `ixi/` are shared under
   CC BY-SA 3.0. Attribution: IXI dataset, Imperial College London / brain-development.org.
2. `celeba/**` — trained on CelebA (Liu, Luo, Wang, Tang, ICCV 2015). CelebA is available for non-commercial research
   purposes only. Weights, figures and metrics in `celeba/` may be used for **non-commercial research only**. No real
   CelebA images are redistributed here.
3. Code (`models/*.py`) — provided as-is for research.

These models are not medical devices and must not be used for diagnosis or clinical decision making.
